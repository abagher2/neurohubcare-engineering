# Independent Providers and Regional Centers: Navigating the Payment Process

> **The Reality for Families & Providers:** When you finally find a tutor, adaptive coach, or therapist who truly connects with your child, hearing *"I'm not vendored with the Regional Center"* can feel like hitting a wall. In practice, SDP and Participant-Directed pathways allow independent providers to get paid without undergoing months of traditional agency vendorization.

Traditional Regional Center vendorization was built for multi-clinician agencies with dedicated billing departments. For an independent speech therapist, specialized swim instructor, or respite worker, traditional vendorization often means months of paperwork and fixed state rate tables.

Fortunately, traditional agency vendorization is not the only way for a qualified provider to work with your family.

---

## How Independent Providers Get Paid Under SDP

Under the **Self-Determination Program (SDP)**, payments flow through the participant's **Financial Management Service (FMS)** rather than directly through the Regional Center's legacy vendor portal.

To get paid smoothly through an FMS on an invoice basis (Bill Payer / 1099 vendor track), an independent provider generally needs:

1. **Business Setup & W-9:** Because an FMS pays independent vendors as businesses on an invoice basis, the provider submits a completed **W-9** (using their business EIN or sole-proprietor Taxpayer Identification Number).
2. **Service Code & Rate Agreement:** The family (often with their Independent Facilitator) maps the provider's services to the appropriate SDP service code and agreed rate inside the certified Spending Plan.
3. **Standardized, Itemized Invoices:** Each invoice must list the participant, dates of service, hours or units delivered, agreed rate, and service description matching the Spending Plan authorization.

By giving independent providers a clear checklist and helping families format clean packets—with automatic multi-month and cross-year service period grouping, cent-reconciled subtotals, and explicit PDF footers distinguishing **"Invoice evidence"** (for unpaid SDP vendor invoices sent to an FMS) from **"Receipt / payment evidence"** (for paid Traditional Regional Center reimbursements)—families can bring the best community specialists onto their Care Team without administrative guesswork.
