# The Anatomy of an IPP Addendum: Documenting Out-of-Network & Specialized Services

> **Grounded Advocacy:** An Individual Program Plan (IPP) is the foundational agreement in a Regional Center case file. When families request an IPP Addendum for a specialized service, success depends on clear alignment between the child's holistic goals, documented generic-resource exhaustion, and provider fit.

When families or Independent Facilitators request funding for a specialized program, adaptive class, or out-of-network support, denials frequently come down to how the request is documented rather than whether the child needs the support.

---

## Understanding the "Generic Resource" Requirement

Under the Lanterman Act, Regional Centers act as the **payer of last resort**. If a requested service could reasonably be provided by a "generic resource"—such as a school district IEP, Medi-Cal, private health insurance, or a standard community program—the Regional Center will require proof that those avenues were explored and could not meet the child's individual needs.

---

## Three Pillars of a Well-Structured IPP Addendum Request

Whether you are preparing for an annual IPP meeting or requesting a targeted addendum, organizing your supporting records in your **Vault** around three pillars makes the review conversation much clearer:

1. **Holistic Child & Family Goal Alignment:** Connect the requested service directly to a measurable developmental, safety, or community-integration goal in the child's IPP or Person-Centered Plan (PCP).
2. **Evidence of Generic Resource Exhaustion:** Keep written documentation—such as insurance denial letters, IEP notes, or waitlist logs from vendored agencies—attached to the request so your Service Coordinator has the audit trail they need.
3. **Specific Provider & Rate Clarity:** Clearly state the provider's qualifications, the service frequency, and the hourly/session rate so the Service Coordinator can take a complete, well-supported package to their internal Purchase of Service (POS) review committee.
