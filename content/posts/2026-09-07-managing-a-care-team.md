# Managing Multiple Providers Without Losing Your Mind

> **The Reality for Families:** One of the greatest strengths of the Lanterman Act—both in SDP and Traditional Social Recreation / Participant-Directed services—is the ability to build a customized care team around your child. The trade-off is managing a patchwork of 1099 contractors, W-2 workers, and community vendors.

When a family moves beyond a single vendored clinic to a personalized network of specialists, the quality and fit of care can improve dramatically. A child's weekly routine might include an independent speech specialist, a 1:1 swim instructor, and a trusted respite caregiver.

With that customization, however, comes a steady stream of administrative coordination.

---

## The Multi-Provider Administrative Load

Every provider type has different payment and compliance requirements:

- **W-2 Co-Employed Workers (Respite / Personal Assistants):** Must log accurate hours within authorized limits before FMS payroll cutoffs, with employer tax and workers' compensation burden accounted for in the Spending Plan.
- **1099 Independent Contractors & Community Vendors:** Must operate as a business entity (with a W-9, EIN/Tax ID, and business invoicing) so the FMS can pay them on an invoice basis, or provide itemized receipts for Traditional SAR reimbursement.
- **Service Code & Rate-Cap Alignment:** Each provider's services must map cleanly to an IPP goal, an authorized service code, and applicable state rate guidelines.

---

## Organizing the Chaos

Families and Independent Facilitators who stay ahead of this workload rely on three core habits:

1. **Separate Passive Records from Active Requests:** Keep long-term foundational documents—IPPs, Person-Centered Plans (PCPs), vendor W-9s, and clinical assessments—organized in a secure **Vault**, while tracking active receipts, invoices, and reimbursements in a dedicated **Action Center**.
2. **Standardize Provider Onboarding:** Clarify upfront whether each provider is billed under **Traditional SAR (Reimburse)** or **SDP (FMS Invoice)** so nobody discovers missing W-9 or business-invoice requirements 60 days later.
3. **Link Every Service to an IPP Goal:** Maintain a clear connection between the child's IPP goals, the authorized service allocation, and every submitted receipt or invoice.
