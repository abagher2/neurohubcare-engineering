# Surviving the Transition to the Self-Determination Program (SDP)

> **The Reality for Families:** Transitioning into California's Self-Determination Program (SDP) offers genuine freedom to choose providers and direct an annual budget—but that flexibility also shifts real accounting, tax-burden, and tracking responsibilities onto the family and their Independent Facilitator.

Transitioning into California's Self-Determination Program (SDP) is often described as liberating. Instead of requesting individual purchase-of-service authorizations for every therapy through a Service Coordinator, participants receive a certified 12-month budget and build a **Spending Plan** tailored to their Person-Centered Plan (PCP) and IPP goals.

However, that autonomy comes with operational complexity that surprises many first-year participants.

---

## The "Blind Budget" Trap

In the Traditional system, the Regional Center tracks service authorizations directly with vendored agencies. If authorized hours run low, the agency is notified directly.

In SDP, funds are administered through a **Financial Management Service (FMS)**. Two common friction points catch families off guard:

1. **Delayed FMS Ledger Updates:** FMS monthly spending reports often lag behind real-time care delivery. If a provider submits invoices late or timesheets are still pending processing, an FMS portal may show an apparent surplus that has already been spoken for.
2. **Gross Wage vs. True Employer Burden:** When hiring W-2 respite workers or personal assistants under a Co-Employer FMS model, employer payroll taxes, workers' compensation, and monthly FMS administrative fees must all be deducted from the certified budget allocation.

---

## Practical Ways to Stay on Track

Navigating SDP smoothly isn't about finding a magic shortcut—it's about having clear visibility into your numbers and records from day one:

- **Real-Time Shadow Tracking:** Track committed service hours, provider rates, and invoice submissions as care happens rather than waiting 30 days for a static FMS statement.
- **Explicit Burden & Fee Math:** Model employer tax/workers' comp burden (`rcMath`) and FMS monthly fees directly inside your Spending Plan so every service code balances accurately before sending updates to the Regional Center.
- **Collaborative Family + IF Review:** Keep the family and Independent Facilitator aligned on the same live budget draft, with explicit parent review and approval before any Spending Plan revision is submitted.
