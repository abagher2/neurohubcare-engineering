# Hiring Direct Respite Workers and Personal Assistants: 1099 vs. W-2 & FMS Rules

> **From Our June 2026 IF Vendorization Interview:** Independent Facilitators told us they spend an enormous amount of time explaining a single rule to families and providers: *under a Bill Payer FMS model, the FMS only pays businesses on an invoice basis—meaning independent providers or personal assistants who aren't W-2 employees must set up as a business (EIN/W-9, business bank account, and itemized invoices).*

One of the most valuable features of Participant-Directed Services (PDS) and the Self-Determination Program (SDP) is the ability to hire people your family already trusts—a skilled respite caregiver, a behavioral aide, or a personal assistant—rather than relying solely on rotating agency staff.

However, getting that person paid smoothly requires understanding how Financial Management Services (FMS) classify workers.

---

## W-2 Co-Employment vs. 1099 Business Invoicing

Depending on the service code and your chosen FMS model, providers are paid through one of two tracks:

### 1. The W-2 Co-Employer Track
- **How It Works:** The family acts as the managing employer (recruiting, scheduling, and approving hours), while the FMS acts as the employer of record (processing W-4s, payroll taxes, withholdings, and workers' compensation insurance).
- **Budget Impact:** Your SDP Spending Plan must reserve enough funds in that service line to cover not only the worker's gross hourly wage, but also the **employer tax and workers' compensation burden** plus monthly FMS fees.

### 2. The 1099 / Business Vendor (Bill Payer) Track
- **How It Works:** The FMS pays independent providers and community vendors strictly on an **invoice basis**.
- **What Providers Need to Know Upfront:** If an independent provider or personal assistant is operating on the 1099/vendor track rather than as a W-2 employee, they must operate as a legitimate business—providing a completed **W-9**, an **EIN** (or business tax identification), a dedicated business invoicing workflow, and invoices aligned with the participant's authorized SDP service code.

Clarifying whether each provider on your Care Team is on a **W-2 Co-Employer**, **1099 SDP Invoice**, or **Traditional SAR Reimbursement** track before they work their first hour prevents weeks of onboarding frustration.
