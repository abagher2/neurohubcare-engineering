# Why Regional Centers Can't Recommend Specific Providers—and How Families Build Trusted Networks

> **From Our Support Group Executive Director Interview:** *"AI can list FMS firms and providers, and it can help organize your paperwork—but technology cannot guarantee which FMS or provider is actually competent, responsive, or right for your child. Be honest about what software can do and where human judgment and community experience matter."*

After receiving a new diagnosis or transitioning into the Self-Determination Program (SDP), almost every parent asks their Service Coordinator the same question: *"Which FMS or therapist do you recommend?"*

Instead of a clear recommendation, families are usually handed a lengthy printout of every vendored provider or FMS in the catchment area.

---

## Why Service Coordinators Can't Endorse One Vendor

Regional Centers are public-benefit agencies bound by strict neutrality and procurement rules. Service Coordinators are generally prohibited from steering families toward a specific private business or FMS agency. At the same time, static PDF vendor lists rarely show which providers have active waitlists, which FMS models they support, or how responsive they are in practice.

---

## What Technology Can—and Cannot—Do

When we built the **NeuroHub Directory** (`/directory`), we took the advice of support group leaders to heart:

- **What Software Does Well:** Searchable directories can organize factual information in one place—service categories, regional coverage, FMS models (Bill Payer vs. Co-Employer), rate structures, and contact details—saving families from hunting across dozens of outdated PDFs.
- **Where Lived Experience Is Irreplaceable:** Listing a provider or FMS in a directory is **not** a guarantee of clinical quality or administrative competence. Families get the best results when they combine structured directory discovery with candid feedback from local parent support groups, Special Education PTAs, and experienced **Independent Facilitators (IFs)** who work with those providers every week.
