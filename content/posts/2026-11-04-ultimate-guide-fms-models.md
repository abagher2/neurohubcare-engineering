# The Complete Guide to FMS Models: Bill Payer vs. Co-Employer vs. Sole Employer

> **Why FMS Selection Matters:** Every Self-Determination Program (SDP) participant must work with a Financial Management Service (FMS) to administer their certified budget. The model you choose determines who you can hire, how taxes and workers' compensation are calculated, and how much monthly administrative fee is deducted from your spending plan.

In California's Self-Determination Program (SDP), the **Financial Management Service (FMS)** serves as the fiscal intermediary between the Regional Center's certified budget and the providers who support your family.

Before finalizing your Spending Plan, it is essential to understand the three FMS models recognized by the Department of Developmental Services (DDS):

---

## 1. The Bill Payer Model (Invoice / 1099 Vendors Only)

- **How It Works:** Neither the FMS nor the participant employs W-2 workers directly. Instead, the FMS pays invoices submitted by businesses, agencies, and independent 1099 contractors.
- **Best For:** Families whose entire care team consists of established clinics, community programs, or independent contractors who operate as a business (with an EIN/W-9 and business invoicing).
- **Budget Impact:** Lowest monthly FMS administrative fee, and no W-2 payroll tax burden—though every provider must be eligible to operate as an independent business vendor.

## 2. The Co-Employer Model (W-2 Employees + 1099 Vendors)

- **How It Works:** Employment responsibilities are shared. The family recruits, schedules, and supervises direct workers (such as respite caregivers or personal assistants), while the FMS acts as the Employer of Record—handling W-4 onboarding, payroll runs, tax withholdings, and workers' compensation insurance. Most Co-Employer FMS agencies can also pay 1099 business vendors via invoice.
- **Best For:** Families hiring direct respite workers, behavioral aides, or personal assistants who do not operate their own independent business entity.
- **Budget Impact:** Higher monthly FMS administrative fee, plus your Spending Plan must allocate funds for **employer payroll taxes, unemployment insurance, and workers' compensation burden** on top of the worker's gross hourly wage.

## 3. The Sole Employer Model

- **How It Works:** The participant or family acts as the legal Employer of Record, obtaining their own employer tax IDs and workers' compensation policies while using the FMS for fiscal processing.
- **Best For:** Less commonly used; typically chosen only when a family wants full legal employer responsibility over a large, dedicated caregiving staff.
